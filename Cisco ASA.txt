Customer replaced their firewall from Cisco ASA to Fortinet FortiGate, the external ADFS servers stopped communicating with the internal servers over the virtual service IP on the load balancer (Aloha/HAProxy). ==========================
Issue Summary:
After migrating the firewall from Cisco ASA to Fortinet Fortigate, the customer’s external ADFS servers were unable to communicate with the internal ADFS servers via the virtual service IP on the load balancer.

Analysis:
The issue likely stems from traffic not reaching the Aloha load balancer or being blocked/altered by the new Fortigate firewall configuration. No logs or traces yet confirm whether packets are arriving at the load balancer.

Next Steps / Solution:

Check Traffic Logs on the Aloha load balancer to verify if traffic is reaching it.
If no logs are seen, inspect Fortigate rules/NAT settings to ensure traffic flow to the virtual service.
If traffic is seen but failing, use packet captures on the listener (if needed) for deeper analysis.=================================================================================================================================================
