What’s happening:

	1. Some TLS connections are being reset (RST) — meaning the connection was suddenly closed.

	2. Other requests show 500 NOSRV errors, which means HAProxy closed the connection because it ran out of buffer
	space when rewriting large HTTP headers (like client certificate data).

Why it happens:

	a. TCP resets → The client tried to reuse a connection that was already closed, or a firewall/load balancer ended it early.

	b. 500 NOSRV → HAProxy’s buffer (tune.maxrewrite) is too small for all the extra headers.

	c. Timeouts → timeout connect 1000ms is too short — it can cut off slow connections early.

Fix plan:

	1. Capture packets (pcap) on both client and HAProxy to see who is sending the RST.

	2. Increase tune.maxrewrite size (e.g., 8K → 16K).

	3. Increase timeout connect to 3–5 seconds.

	4. Adjust client/server timeouts to more practical values.

	5. Re-run the test and compare if resets and 500s drop
